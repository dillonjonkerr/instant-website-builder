Scottsdale, Arizona

The Scottsdale Painting Company That Knows The HOA Rules

Scottsdale is the toughest city in the Valley to paint — not because of the walls, but because of the paperwork. Most communities here run an architectural review committee with an approved palette, and a repaint started without an approved submittal can get red-tagged mid-job. We prepare and file the submittal for you, including body, trim, and pop-out colors with manufacturer codes and LRV values.

On the technical side, Scottsdale’s housing stock is mostly stucco over block or frame, and the west and south elevations take a beating. Homes along the McDowell corridor and up in Troon see reflected heat off granite landscaping that can push surface temps well past air temperature. That changes both which sheen we recommend and which months we’ll agree to spray an exterior.

What Scottsdale Homes Usually Need

  • Stucco crack repair and re-texture — hairline settling cracks bridged before primer, matched to existing knockdown or sand finish.
  • Pop-out and parapet detail — the trim bands and parapet caps that fail first on desert-contemporary elevations.
  • Iron and gate refinishing — courtyard gates, railings, and view fencing rust-treated and recoated.
  • Garage door and front-door refinish — the two surfaces that take the most direct afternoon sun.

Common Questions

Scottsdale House Painting FAQ

Do you handle the HOA approval for my Scottsdale repaint?

Yes, and we’d strongly recommend letting us. We build the submittal package — body, trim, pop-out, and door colors with manufacturer names, codes, and light reflectance values — and file it with your architectural review committee. We don’t schedule spray days until approval comes back in writing, which protects you from a red-tag and a repaint at your own cost.

How much does it cost to paint a house in Scottsdale?

Exterior repaints on typical Scottsdale single-family homes generally land in a wide range depending on square footage, story count, stucco condition, and how much iron and trim detail is involved. A two-story home needing crack repair and elastomeric will sit well above a single-story in sound condition. We measure on site and give you an itemized number rather than a per-square-foot guess.

When is the best time of year to paint an exterior in Scottsdale?

October through April. Once surface temperatures climb past about 90°F the paint film flashes off too quickly to level and adhere properly, which shortens its life significantly. We do book summer exteriors, but we shift to early mornings and chase the shade around the house.

Can you match the desert-contemporary colors used in my community?

Yes. Most Scottsdale communities publish an approved palette, and we can pull from it or colour-match an existing body colour off a clean chip from your wall. During the free color consultation we’ll put large samples up on the actual elevation, because desert light changes a colour dramatically between a fan deck indoors and a west wall at 4pm.
